Alamiyah Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Contact: alamiyahtech@gmail.com

Alamiyah (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a Flutter mobile application that helps people with prayer times, dhikr, dua, Hijri calendar features, Qibla direction, and related Islamic content. This policy explains what information the app may use and why.

1. Information we use

  • Location (approximate or precise). Used on device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction for your chosen place. You may also pick a city without sharing GPS.
  • Notifications. If you enable fasting or other reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device.
  • Saved preferences. Display settings, bookmarks, language, and similar choices are stored on your device.
  • Admin accounts (optional). If you sign in as an admin, we use your email, name, and authentication credentials through Firebase Authentication to manage content.
  • Content and media (admin only). Admins may upload text, images, or videos that are stored with Firebase (Firestore / Storage) so the content can appear in the app.
  • Diagnostics. Standard platform and Firebase services may process device and app diagnostics needed to run and secure the service.

2. How we use information

  • To show prayer times, Hijri dates, Qibla, and library content
  • To send reminders you turn on
  • To keep your bookmarks and display preferences
  • To let authorized admins publish and manage content
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by Google Firebase and related Google services that host authentication, database, and file storage for the app. Home-screen widgets on Android show prayer and reminder content already available in the app; they do not send that content to third parties for advertising.

4. Retention

Preferences and bookmarks stay on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Admin account and published content remain until an authorized admin removes them or the account is deleted. You can request deletion by emailing us or using in-app account deletion when available for admin accounts.

5. Children’s privacy

Alamiyah is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location and notification permissions in system settings
  • Use a city search instead of GPS for prayer times
  • Turn off reminder features
  • Contact us to ask questions or request account or content deletion

7. Security

We use industry-standard services and transport security. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: alamiyahtech@gmail.com
